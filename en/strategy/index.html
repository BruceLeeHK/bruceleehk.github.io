Eight Key Strategies

Each strategy is matched to a specific pest type — based on IPM principles

1. "Luring" Strategy — Cockroach Control

Cockroaches enter homes through drains and cracks. The "Luring" strategy places professional gel bait at strategic entry points and known activity areas. Cockroaches consume the bait, return to the nest, and through the chain effect, eliminate the entire colony.

Target: German & American cockroaches | Effect: 7-14 days for full elimination

2. "Wait at Ease" Strategy — Mosquito Control

Instead of frequent spraying, we install timer-based UV mosquito traps paired with biological larvicide granules. The traps operate during peak mosquito hours, providing long-lasting control without chemical overload.

Target: Aedes albopictus, Aedes aegypti | Effect: Continuous, year-round protection

3. "Remove the Source" Strategy — Rodent Control

Killing rats one at a time is a losing battle. We assess the entry sources, precisely seal holes with professional-grade materials, then deploy IoT smart monitoring boxes to track remaining activity and intercept re-entry attempts.

Target: Rats, mice | Effect: Source-sealed, long-term protection

4. "Strategic Steam" Strategy — Bed Bug Control

Bed bugs reproduce rapidly and hide deep in crevices. We use high-temperature steam (120°C+) to instantly kill adults and eggs in mattress seams, sofa cracks, and baseboard gaps. Paired with specialized residual chemicals for ongoing protection.

Target: Bed bugs | Effect: Same-day knockdown, 30-day residual

5. "Capture the King" Strategy — Termite Control

Termites live in colonies with a queen at the center. To eliminate the colony, we use thermal imaging to locate the nest, then inject slow-acting bait that worker termites carry back to the queen — destroying the colony from within.

Target: Subterranean & drywood termites | Effect: 5-year warranty

6. "Draw Out" Strategy — Powderpost Beetle Control

Powderpost beetles bore into wood and leave tiny round holes with fine wood dust. We apply specialized wood-penetrating chemicals that draw beetles out of their tunnels, eliminating both adults and larvae inside the wood.

Target: Powderpost beetles, wood borers | Effect: Penetrates 5mm into wood

7. "Casual Capture" Strategy — Ant Control

For ants, we use slow-acting sugar-based bait that foraging workers carry back to the colony. Within days, the queen and entire colony are eliminated. We also seal entry points to prevent re-infestation.

Target: Black ants, fire ants, Argentine ants | Effect: Colony elimination in 7-10 days

8. "Remove the Source" Strategy — Wasp & Bee Nest Removal

Wasp nests under eaves or in trees pose serious sting risks. We use professional protective gear and dust-based insecticides applied directly to nest entry points at dusk when wasps have returned — eliminating the entire colony overnight.

Target: Wasps, hornets, bees | Effect: Same-night colony elimination
