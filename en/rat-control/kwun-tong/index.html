Why is Kwun Tong a rat hotspot?

Kwun Tong mixes factory godowns, forty-year-old public estates and new towers: full storage racks at Hung To Road, ageing refuse chutes in Sau Mau Ping and Lam Tin, and URA redevelopment in Yue Man Square displacing rat colonies. Three district-specific blind spots:

  • 🏭 Godown stock & carton stacks — Kwun Tong godowns are stacked with cartons — rats nest inside them and hitch-hike with deliveries straight into offices and homes. It is the district's signature infestation route.
  • 🏢 Ageing estate drainage & chutes — In Sau Mau Ping, Lam Tin and Ngau Tau Kok, worn refuse-chute joints and drainage let rats spread from the top floor downwards, crossing storeys inside the block.
  • 🚧 Redevelopment sites push rats indoors — Large-scale works around Yue Man Square and Kai Tak scatter established colonies into neighbouring homes and commercial blocks — demand for proofing spikes overnight.

How do our rat exterminators end the infestation in Kwun Tong?

Industrial and estate cases each have their own rules: warehouses need stock-safe (non-chemical) methods, estates need block-wide drainage thinking. Our modern approach for Kwun Tong:

  • 🔦 Infrared night-route tracking： Night-vision and fluorescent dust deployed after hours, mapping ratways through carton zones, refuse rooms and risers before we act.
  • 🕸️ Professional rodent mesh sealing： Chute doors, drainage joints, pipe penetrations and shutter bases sealed with stainless steel mesh. Warehouse jobs are always non-toxic — zero contamination of stock.
  • 🌱 IPM rodent management： Stacking and refuse-room management advice plus non-toxic trapping with scheduled reports — suitable for IOs and property management tenders.
