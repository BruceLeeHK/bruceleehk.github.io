Why is Tsuen Wan a rat hotspot?

Tsuen Wan is half dense town (old estates, markets, godowns) and half hillside village (Sham Tseng, Chuen Lung) — country rats come downhill for food while urban rats spread through the drainage. Three blind spots:

  • 🏢 Ageing estate drainage & chutes — In estates from the 1960s–80s around Tai Ho Road, worn refuse-chute and drainage joints let rats move between floors — lower floors get hit first.
  • ⛰️ Hillside village pressure (Sham Tseng / Chuen Lung) — Village houses sit against hillside scrub; wild rats move in for winter. Fruit trees and unsealed compost bins are the granary — once an entry point opens, colonies move in.
  • 🥬 Markets & eatery back lanes — Ample food waste around Tsuen Wan Market and Chung On Street feeds rats daily; at night they climb external pipework into adjoining residential floors.

How do our rat exterminators end the infestation in Tsuen Wan?

Urban and village cases demand different skills: urban jobs focus on drainage spread, village jobs on hillside pressure. Never bait-and-pray — our modern, non-toxic approach for Tsuen Wan:

  • 🔦 Infrared night-route tracking： Night-vision and fluorescent dust record ratways through drainage, refuse rooms and hillside approaches — nests, entry points and chute routes located before we act.
  • 🕸️ Professional rodent mesh sealing： Gullies, chute doors, pipe penetrations and door bases sealed in stainless steel mesh; village jobs include a full garden-perimeter assessment.
  • 🌱 IPM rodent management： Environmental assessment (food waste, clutter, green belts) plus non-toxic physical trapping — safe for people and pets, suitable for IOs and village owners.

Tsuen Wan rat control FAQ

How much does rat control cost in Tsuen Wan?

One-off treatments start around HK$1,500 depending on size and severity; proofing works are quoted in detail after survey. Village jobs include the garden perimeter — WhatsApp first for an indicative price.

Our village is next to the hillside — can rats really be kept out?

Hillside pressure cannot be eliminated, but keeping the house itself rat-free is absolutely achievable: seal every entry point and manage garden food sources (fallen fruit, lidded bins) and the interior stays clean.

Rats in our estate refuse chute — what can be done?

Chutes are the heart of estate infestations. We seal chute doors and drainage joints, advise management on refuse-room housekeeping, and can issue block-wide reports for IO tenders.
