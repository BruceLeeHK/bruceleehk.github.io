"Master, there are ants in the living room, kitchen, bathroom and even the balcony. I've sprayed insecticide every day — why are there more and more?"

Many Hong Kong households assume ants only forage in the kitchen — but once a colony nests inside wall voids or skirting boards, it can spread through the whole flat quickly. Recently our Pest Control Master team received a distress call from an owner of an estate flat in Tsing Yi: large numbers of ants and red ants had appeared in several areas of the unit at the same time, and nothing from the shops had stopped them. They finally decided to bring in a professional team.

1. On-site Survey: Four Living Areas Fallen at Once

On arrival at the Tsing Yi flat, our master carried out a carpet-style ant-trail trace across the whole unit. The infestation was not a single source: it had spread along structural gaps and joints into four main living areas:

  • Living room & skirting boards: ants moving in and out along the wooden skirting and electrical socket gaps, forming a clear two-way "ant highway";
  • Kitchen — the worst hit: large numbers of small red ants gathering under the sink cabinet, around the hob edge and behind the fridge, attracted by moisture and food smells;
  • Bathroom tile joints: worker ants carrying water back to the nest near the shower edge and basin drain;
  • Balcony door frame: outdoor plant pots and the air-conditioner condensate drain acting as a springboard for ants entering the flat.

The owner had sprayed shop-bought insecticide again and again: a batch died on the surface, but a few days later ants reappeared at another corner of the wall — and kept spreading further. This is the classic "the more you spray, the more you get" pattern (the science behind it is explained in Q1 below).

2. Treatment Strategy: Why Not Spray? Professional "Bait Transfer" Instead

For a whole-flat, multi-point outbreak of household ants and red ants, we insist on no blind blanket spraying. Instead we apply a science-based IPM (Integrated Pest Management) plan:

  • Pinpoint the trunk ant trails: the master checked the hidden gaps in the living room, kitchen, bathroom and balcony one by one to find the core routes the worker ants must use — so the gel bait goes exactly where it matters, without contaminating food-prep surfaces or clothing;
  • AFCD-registered professional gel bait: matched to the feeding preferences of red ants and omnivorous household ants, a highly palatable gel bait is applied in droplets along the trails. It is colourless, odourless and safe for people and pets, and worker ants carry it back deep into the nest as if it were high-value food;
  • Domino effect wipes out the queen: the active ingredient has a delayed action, giving worker ants time to feed the bait to larvae and the queen(s) inside the nest. Over the following days the whole colony is eliminated through mutual feeding — the "domino effect" — destroying the nest at its source instead of just repelling ants on the surface.

💡 Master's Notes: Multi-point Outbreak Does Not Mean Multi-point Spraying

The single most important lesson from this case: a "multi-point outbreak" never calls for "multi-point spraying". Ants are social insects — the real battlefield is the queen inside the nest. Whatever method can carry the active ingredient into the nest is the road to eradication. Bait transfer borrows the ants' own "logistics system": worker ants deliver the bait deep into nests that no human could ever find. ❓ [Master Q&A] Home Ant & Red Ant FAQs

Why do shop-bought insecticide sprays or smoke bombs make ants "worse and worse"?

This is the pest-science phenomenon called colony budding. When species like red ants are startled by a repellent insecticide spray, worker ants immediately protect the queens and scatter in every direction. What was 1 nest becomes 3 or 4 new nests in different rooms — and home ant control becomes far harder.

After the bait is applied, why can I still see ants for the first 2–3 days? When will they be gone?

Seeing worker ants busily carrying bait in the first few days is actually an excellent sign! Professional bait relies on workers "living transport" — carrying it deep into the wall void to feed the queen(s). Typically within 3 to 7 days after treatment the domino effect completes: you will see ant numbers crash until they disappear entirely. We never make exaggerated "instant permanent" promises — we follow up on a scientific cycle to secure the result.

I keep seeing tiny, yellowish-brown or winged "ants" at home — how do I tell red ants from termites?

Many Hong Kong owners mistake termites for ordinary ants or red ants — and the wrong product can let termites spread into built-in wardrobes across the whole flat. Check the antennae and waist: ordinary ants have a clearly pinched "wasp waist" and elbowed antennae; termites have a straight "barrel waist" and beaded, straight antennae. For a detailed comparison, see our guide Which Ant Control Company Is Best: Ant vs Termite Identification + Bait Treatment Guide.
