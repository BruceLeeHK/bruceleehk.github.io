🌲 Why are Sha Tau Kok and Ta Kwu Ling village houses so vulnerable to termites?

Village houses along the northern border face three high-risk factors:

  • Surrounded by woodland and wetlands: around Lok Ma Chau, Ta Kwu Ling and Sha Tau Kok, dense vegetation means swarming termites easily fly indoors in late spring and early summer, shed their wings and set up colonies behind skirting boards, in false ceilings or inside door frames.
  • Damp ground floors: village house ground floors (G/F) and gardens trap moisture — timber floors, garden decking and storage rooms become the perfect headquarters for a termite empire.
  • Hidden destruction: termites are "invisible architects" that eat timber from the inside out. By the time floors feel soft or frass drops from door frames, the internal structure is usually already destroyed.
