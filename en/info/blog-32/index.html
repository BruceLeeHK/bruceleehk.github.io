Check 1: Pesticide licence & technician credentials

Start with the licence. A legitimate pest control company will list its registered pesticides (AFCD-registered) and licensed technician details in the written quote. Ask directly: "Do your products have registration numbers? Are your technicians licensed?" Companies that answer promptly and put it in writing earn the next round.

Check 2: Survey before quoting

An instant "all-inclusive" price without seeing the site is the classic trap. The right flow is: WhatsApp photo pre-assessment → on-site survey → written quote. Termite work and rodent proofing must be quoted after a survey — entry points and site difficulty drive the cost. A company willing to survey first is a company whose quote means something.

Check 3: Written, transparent pricing

Verbal quotes invite surprise add-ons. Ask three things: Is your price list public? Does the quote itemise the scope? Any follow-up charges? Pest Control Master quotes in writing via WhatsApp with zero hidden fees — and our full 2026 Hong Kong pest control price list is published for line-by-line comparison.

Check 4: Reports & records

The difference between professional and fly-by-night? Records. Service reports, monitoring data and follow-up logs matter to property managers facing audits, and to homeowners tracking whether the infestation is truly resolved. Our IoT smart rodent boxes with 24-hour monitoring turn "trust me" into "see the data".

Check 5: Warranty & follow-up terms

Colonial pests (bed bugs, cockroaches, termites) rarely die in one visit, so warranty terms decide the real cost. Ask: How long is the warranty? Are follow-up inspections included? What happens if they return? Our bed bug treatment includes two follow-ups within 60 days; termite treatment carries a warranty re-inspection. "One-off" quotes with no warranty often mean paying twice.
