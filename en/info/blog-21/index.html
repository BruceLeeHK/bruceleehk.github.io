❓ Tsuen Wan Rat Infestation FAQ

Spotted rat signs near your home — what can you do right now?

Check hidden kitchen corners, false ceilings and pipe edges immediately for black pellet-like droppings or gnaw marks; keep bins lidded and smoke doors closed. If you spot any sign of rats entering your flat, act early — the longer a rat problem is left, the harder and costlier it usually becomes. Photograph the evidence first, then get a professional rat control company to assess via WhatsApp and ask for an itemised quote.

Rats in the flat — can I catch them myself?

Shop-bought glue traps and snap traps help with small-scale problems, but if the source is in shared spaces — refuse rooms, light wells, external pipes — DIY only treats the symptom. Report common-area issues to the Owners' Corporation or building management as well. A professional's three steps — full inspection, source proofing, monitored trapping — aim to reduce the chance of rats coming back.

How much does rat control cost in Tsuen Wan? Any hidden fees?

Rat control pricing depends on flat size, infestation severity and the amount of proofing work. Pest Control Master offers instant WhatsApp photo-based estimates. The quote lists the service scope, materials and fees before any work starts. For urgent cases we arrange attendance as soon as practicable, subject to technician availability.

Why don't professionals recommend rat poison?

Poisoned rats typically die inside ceilings, wall cavities or drains, causing horrific stench and maggot crises; baits also pose poisoning risks to children and pets. The modern professional approach prioritises IoT smart monitoring + steel-mesh source proofing + physical trapping, reducing risks to children and pets and lowering the chance of rats dying in hidden corners of your home.
