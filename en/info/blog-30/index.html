📖 Why Correct Identification Comes First

The first principle of IPM (Integrated Pest Management) is accurate identification — every later decision (method, target zone, whether proofing is needed) builds on knowing the species. The cost of getting it wrong is very real:

  • Wrong chemistry: repellent sprays on bed bugs scatter the colony — the battlefield grows from one bed to a whole flat;
  • Lost golden window: termites mistaken for ants can gut timber structures in two more months;
  • Wasted money: wrong products, wrong DIY — most people end up calling a professional anyway, at a higher total cost.

So when unsure, the cheapest move is always: confirm first. And nowadays confirming one insect costs exactly one photo.

🍳 Kitchen and Drain Group: German Roach, American Roach, Pharaoh Ant, Drain Fly

The kitchen is pest ground zero, and four regulars each hold their own turf: German cockroaches favour warm, damp harbourages near food — cabinet voids and appliance undersides; coffee-ground frass means the colony is close. American cockroaches live in drains and climb up at night through floor drains and gullies — large, fast, capable of short glides. Pharaoh ants march in trails following sweets and grease — when you see a trail, follow it back to the entry point. Drain flies breed inside drains; their fuzzy, moth-like wings and slow flight give them away, and their presence signals organic build-up in the drain — flushing the trap and gully is the basic fix.

🛏️ Bedding and Body Group: Bed Bug, Flea, Booklice

This group affects sleep and skin: bed bugs bite in lines or clusters on exposed skin (arms, neck, face), backed up by blood specks on sheets and ink-dot faecal spots at frame seams; fleas bite around the ankles (they do not jump high) — homes with pets or carpets should take note; their jumping separates them easily from slow-moving bed bugs. Booklice do not bite at all — they are living "hygrometers": tiny pale insects erupting on newly renovated walls or under wallpaper signal humidity, and the fix is dehumidifying below 60% RH plus removing mould — not constant spraying.

🏗️ Structure and Outdoor Group: Termite, Rat, Wasp

Termites reveal themselves through mud tubes, swarmers and hollow-sounding timber; the core of control is locating and eliminating the nest, not surface spraying. Rats are usually seen as traces rather than animals — spindle droppings, gnaw marks, grease runways and midnight ceiling noises are the standard evidence set; control needs both proofing and monitoring. Wasps and hornets build paper nests under eaves, at AC unit bases and balcony corners, with nest-seeking strongest in autumn — never spray them or burn the nest yourself: a coordinated swarm attack is extremely dangerous. Keep your distance and call professionals promptly; see our wasp and nest removal service page for safety details.

❓ 常見問題 FAQ

What are the most common household pests in Hong Kong?

German cockroaches lead kitchens (warm damp cabinet and appliance voids); bed bugs lead bedrooms (linked to travel luggage and second-hand furniture); American cockroaches and drain flies come from drainage; newly renovated humid flats see booklice; summer and autumn bring ants and wasps. Start with a free AI image ID, then treat by species.

How do I tell a cockroach from a bed bug?

Shape and location: German cockroaches are 1.1–1.6cm, light brown with long antennae and a wing cover, active in kitchens; bed bugs are 4–5mm apple-seed shaped, wingless, found at bed frame seams and on sheets, with line-shaped bites and ink-dot faecal spots. A simple shortcut — bed bugs do not sprint; cockroaches vanish the moment lights come on.

What are the tiny white specks crawling on my wall?

Most likely booklice — 1–2mm, pale translucent, like moving dust, erupting on humid newly renovated walls and under wallpaper where they feed on mould and organic debris. They do not bite. The fix is dehumidifying below 60% RH and removing mould sources, not spraying. Photograph them for AI confirmation if unsure.

I hear running sounds in the ceiling at midnight — rats?

Very likely. Cross-check: spindle-shaped black droppings (6–12mm, pointed ends) on cupboard tops or ceiling gaps, gnaw marks on cables or furniture, grease smudge runways along skirting. Any one of the three is strong evidence. Do not start with poison — a rat dying inside the ceiling is the real nightmare; see the rodent control guide for proofing plus physical capture.

After identifying the pest, what is the next step?

Treat by species: cockroach colonies need gel bait, not spray; bed bugs need 180°C steam plus follow-ups; termites need microwave nest detection; rats need entry-point proofing plus monitoring.
