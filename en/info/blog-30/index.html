Woke up with itchy red welts on your arm and neck — mosquitoes? Or bed bugs (locally called "wood lice" in Cantonese) feeding at night? The trickiest part of bed bug bites is the delayed reaction: welts can appear 1–3 days after the actual feeding, so people blame the trip they took last weekend or the new bed sheet — anything but the mattress. And while you guess, the colony breeds: each female lays several eggs a day and hundreds in a lifetime. Wait two weeks and the treatment zone grows from one bed to the whole room. Start from the bite features, then gather hard evidence with 3 self-checks.

🩸 The 4 Tell-tale Features of Bed Bug Bites

  • Lines or clusters — the classic "breakfast, lunch, dinner" triple, along one line or grouped; mosquito bites scatter randomly;
  • Delayed reaction — the feeding itself is often painless; welts surface 1–3 days later, so the day you see bites is not the day you were bitten;
  • Exposed skin only — face, neck, arms and ankles; areas covered by pyjamas are usually spared;
  • Highly individual reactions — some people react strongly, others barely at all; "no bites" does not mean "no bed bugs" — the bedside evidence decides.

🔍 Self-check #1: Blood Spots and Black Frass on Sheets

Hold the sheet up to the light: rust-red specks are engorged bugs crushed overnight; black pinpoints are droppings that smear like ink when wet — wipe with a damp tissue and a brown-black smear confirms activity. Add pale-yellow translucent moults and you have a solid identification.

🔍 Self-check #2: Mattress Seams and Frame Joints

By day, bed bugs hide in mattress piping, under labels, bed-slat joints, and the screw holes behind headboards. Run a stiff card along the seams: adults are 4–5mm, flat and apple-seed shaped (dark red when engorged); nymphs are translucent and smaller than a sesame seed; eggs are 1mm pearl-white grains clustered in the seams. Wooden frame tenons and the openings of spring boxes are hotspots.

🔍 Self-check #3: The Perimeter Spread

Once numbers grow, bed bugs fan out from the headboard: skirting-board cracks, behind socket covers, wardrobe bottom boards, bedside table drawers, even behind wall pictures. The later you act, the larger the inspection and treatment zone — which is exactly why you should self-check the moment bites appear.

🧳 Just Back From Travel? 4 Anti-bed-bug Habits

  • In hotels, keep luggage on the rack, never on the bed; check the headboard and mattress seams on arrival;
  • Wash travel clothes hot + tumble-dry high (above 60°C kills bugs and eggs);
  • Vacuum suitcase seams and sun- or steam-treat them;
  • Suspect you brought one home? Isolate the suitcase in the bathtub or on tiles — smooth surfaces are hard for bed bugs to climb — and inspect immediately.

❓ Bed Bug Bite FAQ

How do I tell bed bug bites from mosquito bites?

Bed bugs: lines or clusters, delayed 1–3 days, exposed skin only. Mosquitoes: random, immediate, gone within hours. Bites only suggest — the blood spots and frass by the bed decide.

I have bites but can't find any bugs — should I ignore it?

No. Daytime hiding is extremely discreet and nymphs are smaller than sesame seeds. Keep checking nightly with the 3 tricks, or upload bite and room photos to the AI diagnostician for a first-pass assessment.

Are "wood lice" and bed bugs the same thing?

Yes. The local Cantonese name "wood lice" (木蝨) refers to bed bugs (Cimex lectularius) — nothing to do with timber; they simply favour wooden bed frames and furniture. Treatment is the same heat + residual protocol.

Can I just spray insecticide myself?

Not recommended. Common sprays are repellent — they drive bed bugs into sofas, sockets and wall voids, making treatment harder. Professional control combines steam heat, residual sprays and gap dusts.

How many treatments does bed bug control take?

Usually 2+ visits about 10–14 days apart, so newly hatched nymphs are eliminated; severe cases are assessed after survey. Your technician provides a preparation checklist (declutter, hot-treat laundry, etc.).

How do I avoid bringing bed bugs home?

Luggage on the rack in hotels, hot-wash everything on return, vacuum suitcase seams, and inspect second-hand furniture before it enters the house. Full checklist in our home bed bug guide.
