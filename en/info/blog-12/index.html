2. Why Quotes for the Same Service Can Differ Several-Fold

The most common question: "Company A quoted HK$1,500, Company B quoted HK$4,000 — why?" A pest control quote is made up of four cost components. Understand them and you will immediately know which quotes are reasonable and which are inflated:

  • Labour & experience: A seasoned technician pinpoints the source of the infestation and solves it quickly with minimal pesticide; inexperienced operators spray blindly and the problem resurfaces — costing you more in the long run.
  • Chemicals & equipment: Registered pesticides, professional sprayers, heat treatment machines and radar detection devices vary hugely in cost. Companies using cheap, unverified chemicals can quote "low" — but the safety risks are completely different.
  • Technology & inspection: Traditional companies rely on the naked eye; high-tech pest control companies use AI image recognition and Termatrac T3i microwave detection to locate nests precisely, reducing drilling, demolition and repeat treatments.
  • After-sales warranty: Follow-up visits, warranty length and recurrence handling all cost money. Some companies quote low because it's "one visit, no warranty" — and charge again when pests return.

In short: the cheapest pest control company is rarely the best value. True value = transparent pricing + targeted treatment + after-sales accountability.

3. The 5 Hidden-Fee Traps (Anti-Scam Core)

Based on complaints collected across Hong Kong, these are the 5 most common traps. Verify every one of them before signing:

  • Trap 1: Bait pricing, upsell on site — quoted HK$800 by phone, then told on arrival "your case is complicated" and pushed to several thousand. A professional pest control company assesses the infestation BEFORE quoting and confirms the final price in writing.
  • Trap 2: Infestation scare tactics — "The termites have already eaten your whole flat — you must reopen every wall!" If you hear fear-based selling, demand evidence (photos, detection data) and get a second opinion.
  • Trap 3: No follow-up included — termites and bed bugs rarely die off in one visit. Always ask: how many follow-ups are included? How long is the warranty? What happens if they return?
  • Trap 4: "Chemical upgrade" charges — the quote uses a "basic chemical", then on site they claim it won't work and upsell a "German-grade formula" at extra cost. Reputable companies list the chemicals in the written quote.
  • Trap 5: Unlicensed operators — under Hong Kong's Pesticide Ordinance, applying pesticides requires a licence from the AFCD. No matter how cheap an unlicensed operator is, you have zero protection if anything goes wrong.

5. The 5-Point Anti-Scam Checklist for Choosing a Pest Control Company

Finally, here are the 5 core criteria. Whether you found a company by searching "pest control company recommendation" or via a friend's referral, screen them against all five:

  • 1. Licence — confirm a pesticide licence issued under Hong Kong's Pesticide Ordinance (AFCD). Ask for the licence number directly.
  • 2. Diagnostic capability — do they inspect before quoting? Do they use instruments (T3i radar, AI recognition)? A company willing to invest time in diagnosis is a company serious about solving your problem.
  • 3. Written transparent quote — the quote must list scope, number of visits, chemicals used and warranty period. Written confirmation on WhatsApp is the minimum; never rely on verbal promises.
  • 4. After-sales warranty — termites and bed bugs always need follow-up. Ask how many re-inspections are included, the warranty length, and the recurrence policy — in writing.
  • 5. Genuine reviews — read Google reviews and real user posts. Watch for complaints like "surprise charges" or "ignored us when it returned", and see how the company responds to negative feedback.

Pest Control Prices & Selection FAQ

The questions Hong Kong customers ask most when searching for a pest control company — answered by PEST CONTROL MASTER:

How much does a pest control company in Hong Kong cost in 2026?

Depending on pest type and flat size, a single service runs roughly HK$750 to HK$8,000: cockroaches/ants HK$900-1,800, rodents HK$1,900-4,000, termites HK$2,800-8,000, bed bugs HK$3,000-5,800+. Colonial pests need follow-up visits — always confirm warranty terms before signing.

How do I avoid being overcharged by a pest control company?

Three steps: 1) require a written quote listing scope, visits, chemicals and warranty; 2) approve any add-ons in writing only; 3) be wary of bait pricing — quotes far below market rate usually turn into on-site 'complication' surcharges.

Does the price include after-sales warranty?

A professional pest control company always includes follow-up for colonial pests like termites and bed bugs, since they rarely die off in one visit. Ask: how many re-inspections are included? How long is the warranty? Any charges if pests return? Get it all in the written quote.

Why is termite treatment more expensive?

Termite colonies are hidden and highly destructive; the work involves detection, drilling/injection or baiting systems, and multiple monitoring visits. Termatrac T3i microwave radar locates nests non-destructively, so every treatment lands precisely where it matters.

Is high-tech pest control (AI + T3i radar) more expensive?

No. PEST CONTROL MASTER's AI pest identification and WhatsApp estimates are free, and T3i detection is included in our termite plans. High tech means precise diagnosis and targeted treatment with fewer repeat visits — total cost is usually LOWER than traditional blind spraying.

How do I verify a pest control company's licence?

Under Hong Kong's Pesticide Ordinance, applying pesticides requires an AFCD licence. Ask the company for its licence number or verify on the AFCD website. Unlicensed operators offer zero protection if chemicals are misused or the infestation worsens.

Is same-day service really available?

Yes. PEST CONTROL MASTER covers all 18 districts of Hong Kong. After confirming the infestation and quote on WhatsApp, same-day on-site service can usually be arranged; urgent cases (bed bug bites, flying termites) are prioritised.

How do I get the most accurate pest control quote?

Provide three things: 1) photos or video of the pest (or use AI diagnosis); 2) approximate flat size and layout; 3) where the pests appear, how long it's been going on, and whether you've sprayed insecticide yourself. The more complete the information, the more accurate the quote — and the less room for surprise charges.
