In Hong Kong, rat control and pest control may look deceptively simple — just lay down some rat poison and spray a bit of insecticide, right? In reality, professional pest control is far more complex than most people imagine and demands specialist knowledge and skills. The Hong Kong SAR Government sets clear qualification requirements and a regulatory framework for pest control practitioners, ensuring they have adequate professional competence and safety awareness.

The 5 Golden Rules for Choosing a Professional Pest Control Company

Golden Rule 1: A Valid Statutory Licence and FEHD Registration

Under Hong Kong's Pesticides Ordinance (Cap. 133), anyone who deals in pesticides in the course of business must hold a Pesticide Licence issued by the Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department (AFCD). In addition, the Food and Environmental Hygiene Department (FEHD) also applies relevant registration and regulatory requirements to pest control companies. When choosing a pest control company, the very first step is to confirm that it holds a valid licence.

Golden Rule 2: Adoption of an IPM (Integrated Pest Management) System

A quality pest control company does not rely solely on traditional chemical spraying; it adopts the internationally recognised IPM (Integrated Pest Management) approach. Through environmental monitoring, sealing off pest pathways, physical trapping and precisely placed gel baits, IPM cuts off the pests' food chain and breeding routes at the source.

Golden Rule 3: Pesticide Safety and Transparency (a must-ask if you have children or pets)

A professional pest control company should proactively provide Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) for the pesticides used, and apply low-toxicity, odourless or safe biological agents registered with Hong Kong's AFCD, ensuring the treatment process is completely safe for young children, pregnant women and pets at home.

Golden Rule 4: Transparent Pricing — Avoid Wildly Fluctuating Quotes and Hidden Charges

A reputable company will provide a detailed on-site assessment and a standard written quotation before any work begins, clearly stating the number of visits, the scope of coverage and the warranty period. Never choose a company that quotes an extremely low price over the phone only to add on all sorts of extra charges afterwards.

Golden Rule 5: A Clear After-Sales Warranty Commitment

Stubborn pests such as bed bugs or termites usually require multiple re-inspections and repeated treatments. A professional company will offer a warranty period of 1 to 6 months; if the pests return within the warranty period, a free on-site follow-up visit is provided.
