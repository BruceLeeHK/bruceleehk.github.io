🪳 Cockroach Control (German & American)

Cockroaches are among the most common household pests in Hong Kong — not only causing disgust but more importantly carrying pathogens that pose serious health threats to your family. The two most common species in Hong Kong are German and American cockroaches, each with different habits requiring different control methods.

German cockroaches are smaller and typically found in kitchens, bathrooms, and other warm, humid areas. They reproduce extremely fast — one female can produce dozens of nymphs in a short time. American cockroaches are larger and mainly active in drains, garbage rooms, and outdoor gardens. Both contaminate food and spread disease.

Our cockroach control uses a "chain baiting" strategy with professional gel bait and residual spray, combined with Insect Growth Regulators (IGR) to break the reproductive cycle at the source. We don't just spray the surface — we carefully inspect every corner of your home to find hiding spots and entry routes, then apply targeted treatment. After service, we provide detailed prevention tips to keep cockroaches from returning.

🕷️ Bed Bug Control

Bed bugs are flat, oval-shaped, reddish-brown insects about 5-7mm in length. They hide in bed frame crevices, sofa seams, and near electrical outlets, accompanied by black blood-spot excrement. Their rapid reproduction makes bed bugs a high-stress pest for many Hong Kong families.

Our bed bug treatment uses a dual approach: high-temperature steam (kills adults and eggs through heat) plus specialized residual chemicals (provides long-lasting protection). This combined method achieves thorough control with minimal disruption to your home.

Pricing Reference

  • Residential (2+ bedrooms): from HK$2,200
  • Steam + chemical dual treatment
  • 30-day warranty included

🐜 Termite Control

Termites are a serious threat to Hong Kong homes — they feed on wood and can cause structural damage if left untreated. Common signs include mud tubes (earthen tunnels along walls), hollow-sounding wood, and discarded wings near windows.

Our termite control uses thermal imaging to accurately locate hidden nests inside walls and wooden structures without damaging decor. We then inject long-lasting protective agents to create a barrier that prevents re-infestation for years.
