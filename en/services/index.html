🪳 Cockroach Control (German & American)

Cockroaches are among the most common household pests in Hong Kong — not only causing disgust but more importantly carrying pathogens that pose serious health threats to your family. The two most common species in Hong Kong are German and American cockroaches, each with different habits requiring different control methods.

German cockroaches are smaller and typically found in kitchens, bathrooms, and other warm, humid areas. They reproduce extremely fast — one female can produce dozens of nymphs in a short time. American cockroaches are larger and mainly active in drains, garbage rooms, and outdoor gardens. Both contaminate food and spread disease.

Our cockroach control uses a "chain baiting" strategy with professional gel bait and residual spray, combined with Insect Growth Regulators (IGR) to break the reproductive cycle at the source. We don't just spray the surface — we carefully inspect every corner of your home to find hiding spots and entry routes, then apply targeted treatment. After service, we provide detailed prevention tips to keep cockroaches from returning.

🕷️ Bed Bug Control

Bed bugs are flat, oval-shaped, reddish-brown insects about 5-7mm in length. They hide in bed frame crevices, sofa seams, and near electrical outlets, accompanied by black blood-spot excrement. Their rapid reproduction makes bed bugs a high-stress pest for many Hong Kong families. Our bed bug heat treatment Hong Kong service combines high-temperature steam with precision chemical treatment to eradicate eggs and adults in one programme.

Our bed bug treatment uses a dual approach: high-temperature steam (kills adults and eggs through heat) plus specialized residual chemicals (provides long-lasting protection). This combined method achieves thorough control with minimal disruption to your home.

🐜 Termite Control

Termites are a serious threat to Hong Kong homes — they feed on wood and can cause structural damage if left untreated. Common signs include mud tubes (earthen tunnels along walls), hollow-sounding wood, and discarded wings near windows. 🐝 Wasp & Hornet Nest Removal ("ma fung")

In Hong Kong people say "ma fung" (馬蜂) or "wong fung" (黃蜂) — both point to the same aggressive nest-building wasps whose paper nests appear on canopies, AC tops and garden trees. Disturbed colonies attack in swarms, so never knock the nest down, burn it or hose it. Our team arrives with professional protective gear: species ID (hornet/wasp vs bee — bee nests are referred to beekeepers first) → risk assessment → safe removal with residue cleanup → re-nesting prevention (gap sealing + seasonal checks). Pricing depends on nest size and location; written quote after site survey.

Pest Control Services FAQ

How do I choose a good pest control company in Hong Kong?

Verify four things: whether the pesticides are AFCD-registered; whether they survey first and issue a written quotation; whether treatment comes with photo records; and whether a warranty-period follow-up is included. We do all four — and make case records available for verification.

Which areas do you cover? Do you serve Stanley and Causeway Bay?

All 18 districts of Hong Kong — from Stanley and Causeway Bay to Kennedy Town on Hong Kong Island, across Kowloon and the New Territories, plus outlying islands such as Discovery Bay and even Sha Tau Kok Frontier Closed Area cases (Closed Area Permit required). For remote spots, send us your address on WhatsApp for scheduling.

How is pest control priced?

Past-case reference ranges: termite ~HK$2,800–$8,000; bed bugs ~HK$3,000–$5,800; cockroaches ~HK$900–$1,800, depending on scale and workload. We survey first and issue a written quotation stating scope and warranty. See our 2026 pest control pricing guide for the full picture.

Are treatments safe for children and pets?

We use AFCD-registered pesticides applied in targeted ways — gel baiting, crack-and-crevice injection — to minimise contact risk; application points and precautions are explained before treatment.

Will pests come back? Is there a warranty?

Pest activity depends on humidity, hygiene and nearby sources, so recurrence is possible. We follow up within the warranty period — if activity reappears at the same spot, re-inspection and treatment are free, with prevention advice provided.

How do I get started?

Send photos via WhatsApp for a preliminary assessment, or use our free AI pest diagnosis to identify the pest instantly. After confirming the approach, we schedule a survey and issue a written quotation. For urgent cases, mention it in your message and we will prioritise where we can.
