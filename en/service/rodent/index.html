Our Process — Four Documented Steps

Rodent control is not "put down poison and hope". Our process runs in four documented steps, each with records and photos:

  • Step 1 | 📲 WhatsApp enquiry — Send photos or videos of the signs (where, how often, gnaw marks or droppings); we reply quickly with a preliminary price range and visit timing.
  • Step 2 | 🔍 On-site survey — Identify the species (Norway rat / roof rat / house mouse), track pathways with night vision, and mark every entry point: drains, pipe penetrations, door gaps, ceiling openings.
  • Step 3 | 🛡️ Source sealing + trapping — Structural sealing with stainless-steel mesh plus physical traps; if rodenticide is truly needed, AFCD-registered products in locked bait stations only.
  • Step 4 | 📡 IoT monitoring & follow-up — Smart rat boxes log activity; scheduled re-checks top up sealing; during the warranty period re-visits at the same spots are free.

IoT Smart Rat Box — Data-driven Monitoring

The hardest part of traditional rodent control is verification — after the technician leaves, are the rats really gone? IoT smart rat boxes answer with data:

Restaurant & Kitchen Rat Control: IoT Smart Monitoring + IPM

Why are restaurant kitchens prone to rats? Water, food sources and hiding gaps — rear staircases, storage rooms and pipe runs all serve as rat highways. Rat sightings and complaints can trigger FEHD follow-up that affects more than one day of business; for chains, brand reputation is at stake too.

We provide IPM (Integrated Pest Management) for F&B operators: survey and map rat activity routes and entry points → stainless-steel mesh sealing → targeted bait boxes and traps → 24-hour IoT smart rat box monitoring with periodic reports, ready for management offices, auditors or headquarters records.

Rat Control Company FAQ

How much does a rat control company in Hong Kong charge? Any mid-way price hikes?

Pricing depends on floor area, infestation severity and proofing workload: past cases for single-visit removal with assessment generally fall in HK$1,500–$3,800 per visit; proofing works are quoted as a fixed price after survey, with items and warranty listed in the contract — no surprise add-ons afterwards. Actual pricing is subject to the written quotation.

How do I choose a reliable rat control company?

Five checks: ① a valid pesticide licence ② IPM source sealing (not just poisoning) ③ verifiable real cases ④ Google rating and review content ⑤ whether the quote states warranty period and follow-up counts. See our 2026 selection guide for details.

Will you use rat poison? Is it safe with kids and pets at home?

We lead with non-toxic physical options: night-vision tracking, physical trapping and source sealing. Indoor rodenticide is not the first choice unless the infestation truly requires it; if used, only AFCD-registered products in locked bait stations to reduce exposure risks.

What is the process and how fast are the results?

Process: WhatsApp photo enquiry → on-site survey (species / pathways / entry points) → source sealing + trapping → IoT monitoring follow-up. Depending on severity, activity generally tapers after proofing is completed; re-entry risk depends on the environment and any new gaps — free re-checks within the warranty period.
