Cockroach Treatment Tech — Colony Action, Not Spot Sprays

German cockroaches breed fast and resist many sprays; a spray only handles the ones you see. Our approach targets the colony:

  • Species Identification — German cockroaches: small, hiding in cabinets and appliances, breeding fast. American cockroaches: large, entering via drains. Right ID, right chemistry.
  • Gel Bait Mechanics — Gel applied into harbourage gaps is carried back to the nest — colony-level impact beyond contact kills.
  • Sanitation — Cut water and food sources (leaks, grease, overnight food, open bins) so bait works better and results last longer.
  • Monitoring Follow-up — Monitor traps track activity density + scheduled re-baits — confirm the downward trend with data.

Cockroach Control Company FAQ

Why are cockroaches so hard to get rid of yourself?

German cockroaches breed fast and resist many sprays; a spray only handles the roaches you see while the colony keeps breeding in cabinet hinges and appliances. Gel baiting works because roaches carry it back to the nest — that is colony-level control.

How much does cockroach control cost in Hong Kong?

Single treatments about HK$900–$1,800 per visit (past-case reference) depending on size and infestation level; commercial kitchens quoted after survey; maintenance contracts on annual terms via WhatsApp. Final pricing subject to written quotation.

German cockroach vs American cockroach?

German cockroaches are small (about 1.5 cm), hide in cabinets and appliances and breed very fast; American cockroaches are large (about 4 cm) and usually enter via drains. Harbourages and treatments differ — identify at survey first, then apply the right chemistry.

Is gel bait safe with children and pets?

We use AFCD-registered pesticides applied as gel dots into harbourage gaps rather than spraying everywhere, reducing exposure risks for children and pets; application points are explained so you can plan cleaning.

What can be done for restaurant kitchens?

Hotspot treatment + maintenance contracts: scheduled visits, re-baits and monitor-trap reports aligned with FEHD hygiene requirements; treatments can be scheduled outside opening hours to reduce operational impact.

Cockroaches all over the refuse room?

Refuse rooms are common areas — control falls under IO/management duties, while unit interiors are the owner's arrangement. We can coordinate with the management office on common-area treatment so treated units are not re-invaded from public scope.

How is German cockroach treatment different from American cockroach control?

German cockroaches are small, fast-breeding and nest inside kitchen cabinets and appliance gaps — treated mainly with gel baiting. American cockroaches are larger and invade via drains and refuse rooms, so treatment adds drain work and sealing. Surveys determine the right plan and quotation for each.
