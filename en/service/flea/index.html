Bed Bug Treatment Process — Four Steps, Fully Documented

The worst bed bug "treatment" is a quick spray. Our four-step process covers adults through the egg hatching window:

  • Step 1 | 📲 WhatsApp Photo Enquiry — Photograph bite marks, blood spots or traces (seams, bed board underside) — prompt reply with initial direction and scheduling.
  • Step 2 | 🔍 On-site Inspection — Point-by-point check of seams, bed boards, sofas, skirting and socket points to map infestation extent and stage (adults / eggs / spread).
  • Step 3 | 🛡️ Treatment — High-temp steam on seams and bed boards + crack-and-crevice application (AFCD-registered pesticides) + bed interceptors deployed.
  • Step 4 | 🔍 Follow-up — Two free follow-ups within 60 days covering the egg hatching window; interceptor monitoring until activity is stable.

Bed Bug Treatment Tech — Physical + Chemical Combo

Bed bugs hide in many spots (seams, board folds, skirting, sockets) and eggs hatch over time — a single method is rarely enough. Our combo:

  • High-temp Steam — Steam treatment on seams and board folds — physical action on adults and eggs, reducing chemical reliance.
  • Crack-and-Crevice Application — Precise application into frames, skirting and furniture gaps (AFCD-registered pesticides) — targeting harbourages instead of spraying everywhere, safer around children and pets.
  • Bed Interceptors — Interceptor cups under bed legs monitor activity — if bed bugs climb, you know; follow-ups backed by data.
  • 60-day Follow-up Cycle — Bed bug eggs hatch over a window — two follow-ups within 60 days cover the cycle for a more solid outcome.

Bed Bug Control Company FAQ

Are "wood bugs" and bed bugs the same thing?

Yes — the Cantonese nicknames (muk sat, chong sat) all refer to bed bugs. Fleas (cat fleas), silverfish and booklice are different pests with different treatments — send photos via WhatsApp or free AI diagnosis to identify first, then treat correctly.

How much does bed bug treatment cost in Hong Kong?

Spot treatments from about HK$1,200; whole-unit treatment about HK$3,000–$5,800 (past-case reference) depending on size, extent and furniture. Our standard service includes two free follow-ups within 60 days; final pricing subject to written quotation after survey.

Do I have to throw away my mattress?

Not necessarily. Steam + crack-and-crevice + interceptors can usually keep the mattress; replacement is only suggested for severe or damaged cases. The on-site assessment gives an honest recommendation — we will not upsell.

How many treatments are needed?

Bed bug eggs hatch over a window — typically two follow-ups within 60 days after the initial treatment to cover the cycle. Our standard service already includes them; services without follow-ups carry a much higher relapse risk.

How do I avoid bringing bed bugs home from travel?

Inspect seams and bed boards on check-in (look for black faecal dots and blood spots), keep luggage off the floor, and hot-wash and hot-dry clothes at 60°C when home. See our travel prevention guide for the full routine.

Bed bugs in neighbouring units or common areas?

Bed bugs can spread along wall gaps and corridors. Common areas (corridors, refuse rooms) fall under IO/management responsibilities; we can coordinate with the management office on common-area treatment while treating the affected unit — inside and outside together.
