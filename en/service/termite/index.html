Wood Borer Treatment — How It Differs from Termites

What are wood borers? Wood borers are the larvae of powderpost and false powderpost beetles. They tunnel inside timber, leaving pin-sized exit holes on the surface and powdery frass nearby (like fine sand or ground coffee). Unlike termites, they do not build mud tubes or live in large colonies — damage stays within the infested timber pieces instead of spreading across your home.

Treatment depends on severity: light cases are handled with timber preservative surface treatment and injection to seal the galleries; heavily infested pieces are recommended for replacement, with a check on the source (e.g. furniture or flooring timber already carrying larvae). Hong Kong's humid climate accelerates borer activity, so improving ventilation and moisture control matters too.

Not sure whether it is termites or wood borers? Snap a photo and try our free AI pest diagnosis, or WhatsApp our team — the two pests need different treatment plans and quotations, which will be itemised separately after inspection.

Termite Control Company FAQ

How much does a termite control company in Hong Kong charge?

Past-case references run about HK$2,800–$8,000 depending on infestation extent, nest depth and works volume; some spot treatments from HK$2,800. Quotes are always issued in writing after survey, with scope and warranty stated in the contract. See our 2026 pest control fees guide for the full market picture.

What is Termatrac T3i and why no demolition?

T3i is a microwave radar detector that reads termite activity through walls. No-demolition means you do not have to hack open your renovation to find galleries — precise locating enables focused treatment and avoids unnecessary demolition losses.

How do I know if my home has termites?

Common signs: mud tubes along walls or door frames, bubbling timber surfaces, hollow sounds when tapping wood, and spring-summer swarmers with shed wings. Photograph anything suspicious and WhatsApp us, or try the free AI pest diagnosis first.

Will termites come back after treatment?

Termite activity depends on humidity, timber structures and nearby colonies — some situations can recur. We follow up with warranty-period re-inspections and T3i re-checks: activity found at the same location within warranty is re-treated free, with prevention advice provided.

What chemicals do you use? Is it safe with kids and pets?

We use AFCD-registered pesticides applied in targeted ways (injection / baiting) at defined locations to reduce exposure risks for children and pets; application points and precautions are explained before treatment.

Do you serve restricted areas and outlying islands?

Yes. Restricted-area cases around Sha Tau Kok (closed-area permit zones) are within our track record; Discovery Bay and other islands served as usual. All 18 districts covered — for remote locations, please state your address on WhatsApp for scheduling.

Do you treat wood borers (powderpost beetles)?

Yes. Wood borers are beetle larvae that tunnel inside timber, leaving pin-sized exit holes and powdery frass — unlike termites, they do not build mud tubes. Treatment depends on severity: timber preservative application, with replacement recommended for heavily infested pieces; quoted separately from termite work after inspection.
