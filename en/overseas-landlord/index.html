Three pain points for overseas owners — solved

After emigrating, the hardest part of owning a Hong Kong flat is dealing with pests from half a planet away. Over the years we have handled commissions from Hong Kong owners now living in the UK, Australia and Canada, and the pain points cluster into three types — each with a proven remote workflow that feels like being on site.

1. Tenant complains about bed bugs — you are caught in the middle

A tenant reports bed bugs or termites and demands immediate action; you cannot judge the situation, choose a contractor, or assess compensation claims. We contact the tenant directly, run a free AI photo diagnosis to confirm the species, assess professionally and quote in writing — reports go to both of you with photo evidence, defusing disputes.

2. Sale handover or new lease — pests scare buyers away

Cockroach nests or termite tracks discovered before handover can cut your price or sink the deal; bed bugs before a new tenant moves in can trigger cancellation claims. Our pre-handover deep treatment combines bed bug steam, termite survey, cockroach baiting and rodent proofing as needed, with a completion photo report that reassures buyers and tenants — protecting both rent value and sale confidence.

3. You cannot inspect or supervise in person

The worst fear is a contractor who says "done" after a quick spray. Our standard remote supervision: survey photos + written quotation before work; photos/videos of each key step during work; a treatment-location checklist, chemical safety notes and re-visit schedule after completion — all archived on WhatsApp, traceable, with warranty visits included.

The four-step remote workflow

Everything runs from WhatsApp. We are used to asynchronous communication with the UK (7–8 hours behind) and Australia (2–3 hours ahead): you reply overnight, we act the next Hong Kong morning, and every key decision is confirmed in writing.

  1. WhatsApp request + photos (Day 1)

    Send photos of the signs (droppings, tracks, bites) with the property address and preferred timing. Not sure what the pest is? Try our free 7×24 AI image diagnosis to identify the species first.

  2. Survey arranged + written quote (within 1–3 days)

    We contact the tenant or management office to schedule the survey (for vacant flats we can coordinate with your agent), then issue a written quotation with survey photos via WhatsApp.

  3. Treatment + cloud supervision reports (as scheduled)

    Key steps — steam treatment, crevice injection, bait placement, proofing points — are reported with photos/videos as they happen; locations, chemicals used and safety notes all go into the completion report.

  4. Warranty re-visits + e-receipt (per plan)

    Follow-up visits per plan (e.g. two re-visits within 60 days for bed bugs), each with photo reports; e-receipts sent to your WhatsApp for tax or tenancy records.
