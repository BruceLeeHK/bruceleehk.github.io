About the "AI Taking-Jobs Countdown"

Where does this countdown date come from?
2 October 2035 is a symbolic creative setting we chose — not an official forecast by any institution or study. Global discussion about how AI reshapes jobs has grown in recent years, and we turned that conversation into a playful countdown: rather than worrying, come and experience what smart pest control can already do. In comparable public AI-occupancy research, hands-on service roles tend to be "AI-assisted" rather than "fully replaced" — what really gets replaced is the outdated way of working.
Will traditional pest-control methods really disappear?
Not overnight. Chemical and physical controls remain part of IPM (Integrated Pest Management) — but surveillance and diagnosis have already gone AI: IoT smart rat boxes monitoring 24/7, Termatrac T3i radar seeing termites through walls, thermal-imaging cameras already used in public-health work. What gets obsolete is guesswork, not people.
I'm a pest-control worker myself — what should I do?
Our master puts it simply: it's never AI that takes your job — it's the competitor who knows how to use AI. Inspection tools, monitoring systems, photo-based diagnosis and client communication can all be upgraded. We welcome fellow practitioners to exchange ideas on smart pest control — this industry isn't a zero-sum game; when the customer experience improves, the whole industry grows.
