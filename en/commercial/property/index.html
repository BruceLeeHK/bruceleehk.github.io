Four Major Estate Black Spots — Precisely Targeted

Residential estates are heavily landscaped and typically comprise car parks, refuse rooms (bins) and pet areas. Cable trenches, telecom ducts, foul drains and rainwater pipes interweave between towers — an underground network ideal for rodents to shelter and migrate block to block. On top of that, cockroaches hitch into flats with shopping or during moves, while pet waste and refuse-room residue breed flies and mosquitoes. Once pests travel from common areas into units along drains or cable trenches, owner complaints, OC enquiries and damage to your management grading quickly follow.

🔴 A. Car Parks, Risers & Cable Trenches (Horizontal & Vertical Rodent Corridors)

Challenge: Car-park drains, high-voltage cable rooms and the riser shafts threading between towers form an "underground expressway" for rodents to cross blocks and invade units.

Solution: Source-level physical exclusion with stainless-steel rodent mesh and gnaw-resistant sealant on pipe penetrations and water/electric trenches; IoT smart rodent stations with 24/7 cloud sensing in car parks and common-area risers track movement in real time for precise trapping.

🔴 B. Refuse Rooms, Food-waste Areas & Pet Zones (Fly & Odour Sources)

Challenge: Estate refuse rooms and food-waste bins accumulate organic residue; together with pet waste they are the main breeding ground for flies and drain flies — and a ready source of odour complaints.

Solution: Professional residual spraying and biological drain treatment across refuse rooms and adjoining common areas; dedicated fly-trapping devices near the refuse room monitor and suppress fly density.

🔴 C. Tower Lobbies, Lift Machine Rooms & Switchrooms (Hidden Harborage & Safe Defence)

Challenge: Switchrooms, lift machine rooms and lobby false ceilings are warm and hidden — ideal harbourage for German cockroaches and rodents, and gnawed wiring can trip lifts or power supplies.

Solution: Low-sensitivity, odour-free, low-toxicity gel bait in pin-point placements; non-toxic physical traps in switchrooms and machine rooms so chemicals never threaten critical estate facilities.

🔴 D. Landscaping, Ornamental Pools & Sump Pits (Mosquito Breeding & Larval Control)

Challenge: Shrubby landscaping shelters adult mosquitoes, while car-park sump pits and ornamental-pool standing water breed larvae — Aedes albopictus biting residents.

Solution: Scheduled larvicide (biological) treatment of sump pits and stagnant water to break the breeding cycle; anti-mosquito barrier spraying across landscaped zones plus outdoor glue-board lamps to cut adult mosquito density markedly.

Property Pest Control FAQ

What does a property pest control contract usually include?

Typically: scheduled common-area inspections (refuse rooms, lift shafts, light wells, corridors), rodent proofing works, IoT smart rodent-box monitoring, mosquito and termite control, an e-service report per visit and an annual summary. Scope, frequency, warranty and reporting are stated in the contract — final terms per the written quotation.

How do you fix a refuse room rat problem properly?

Refuse rooms are a rat 'canteen' — proper treatment takes three steps: (1) environmental measures (waste-flow advice, door-gap sealing); (2) structural stainless-steel mesh proofing of building entries; (3) continuous IoT rodent-box monitoring. Poison alone is not a fix — our IPM approach puts exclusion first.

How do IoT smart rodent boxes work?

Each box senses rodent activity and uploads data automatically. Management offices receive monitoring reports showing which locations have activity, how often, and whether proofed entries hold — OC meetings get numbers, not feelings.

Who is responsible for common areas versus individual units?

Under the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance (Cap. 132) framework, common areas are generally the owners' corporation's or management office's responsibility; unit interiors belong to owners or tenants. We can serve both sides and keep the reports separated accordingly.

Can you present the proposal at an OC meeting?

Yes. We provide written proposals, quotations and credential documents for circulation; a specialist can attend to present details. Tender documents can be requested via the form.

How does pricing compare with residential work?

Commercial contracts are quoted by property profile, frequency and monitoring configuration — a different logic from one-off residential jobs, so there is no flat rate card. The principle is the same: survey first, written quotation with itemised scope. Residential reference pricing is in our Pricing Guide.
