Commercial Pest Control FAQ

How is a commercial pest control contract priced?

Commercial contracts are priced on floor area, pest risk, service frequency and monitoring configuration — there is no one-size rate card. Every commercial quotation is survey first, then a written quotation (Quotation) stating scope, frequency, warranty and reporting arrangements — no 'surprise extras', final pricing per the written quotation.

Do you support tender / procurement documents?

Yes. Select 'tender-related information' on the commercial quote form and we will provide company profiles, service proposals, insurance and credential documents. We have applied for listing on the Government Logistics Department and LCSD supplier lists; corporate clients are welcome to request registration documents for procurement evaluation.

Is there a report for every service visit?

Yes. Every visit comes with an electronic service report — inspection points, treatment locations, IoT smart rodent-box monitoring data and follow-up advice — together with the AI-assisted pest diagnosis report and IPM treatment prescription, ready for corporate filing and government inspections.

Are the chemicals you use safe?

We use pesticides registered with the Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department (AFCD); food-safe non-toxic bait systems are available for F&B environments. Our IPM approach puts source exclusion first to reduce chemical reliance — exactly what corporate clients and HACCP audits value.

Who is responsible for common areas versus individual units?

Under the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance (Cap. 132) framework: building common areas (refuse rooms, lift shafts, light wells, corridors, external pipework) are generally the responsibility of the owners' corporation or management office; inside individual units it is the owner or tenant. We can serve both — common-area programmes for the OC and follow-up arrangements for individual residents.

Which areas do you cover?

All 18 districts of Hong Kong and even the Frontier Closed Area — see our documented case at a century-old gold shop in Sha Tau Kok (with a Frontier Closed Area permit). Site surveys can be arranged in Central, Admiralty, Tsim Sha Tsui, Kwun Tong, Tsuen Wan and other business districts.
