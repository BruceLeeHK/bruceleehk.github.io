Commercial Pest Control FAQ

How is a commercial pest control contract priced?

Pricing depends on floor area, pest risk, service frequency and monitoring deployment. We survey first, then issue a written quotation listing scope, frequency, reporting arrangements and fee items — no surprise add-ons afterwards. The written quotation is final.

What reports do you provide for owners' corporations or management?

An electronic service report for every visit, plus AI-assisted pest diagnostic reports and IPM treatment records. IoT smart rat box contract clients also receive regular monitoring data reports — all filed digitally, ready to present to corporations, management or inspectors.

What is an IoT smart rat box and how does it help malls and estates?

It is a 24-hour automatic monitoring device that logs rodent entry and exit times and locations. With trend reports you can act before an infestation escalates. In the Mei Foo Sun Chuen multi-block pipe rodent case, IoT boxes pinpointed the routes before block-by-block exclusion.

Will treatment disrupt our operations or guests?

We schedule around you: after-office hours, nights and holidays. Hotels get discreet service (low-profile equipment, staggered with housekeeping); restaurants can book after closing — protecting your business.

What chemicals do you use? Is food safety affected?

Food environments get non-chemical methods first (exclusion, trapping, environmental management). When pesticides are needed, only AFCD-registered products in lockable bait stations, positioned away from food and prep surfaces. Every service lists materials used on the treatment record.
