Bed Bug Programme — Rapid Response, Discreet Execution

Hotel bed bug control has two fears: incomplete treatment that re-ignites, and guests witnessing the works. Our programme addresses both:

  • Rapid response — WhatsApp live during working hours; a room finding triggers immediate scheduling, no waiting for the next inspection day.
  • Room isolation & adjacent-room checks — affected room sealed immediately; adjacent rooms inspected too (bed bugs spread through wall voids).
  • Steam + crack-and-crevice treatment — high-temperature steam on seams, headboards and furniture gaps, plus targeted application of AFCD-registered pesticides.
  • Follow-up & monitoring — post-treatment re-inspection plus monitoring devices until confirmed clear.

🤫 Discreet service: staggered scheduling (while guests are out or late evening), unbranded equipment on request, fast and quiet works — housekeeping keeps its normal rooming schedule.

Termatrac T3i Radar Detection — Zero Damage to Premium Interiors

Premium hotels dread "inspection that breaks things". Termatrac T3i radar solves exactly that:

  • Radar through-wall detection — reads activity inside walls, timber and furniture surfaces; no demolition, no prying up floors.
  • Premium interiors untouched — marble, timber veneer and custom joinery stay intact; no marks, no holes.
  • Bed bug spot targeting — pinpoints hidden harbourages in wall voids for precise treatment.
  • Termite nests too — the same T3i radar finds termite colonies in timber structures: termite control service →

Housekeeping Bed Bug Training — Every Staff Member Is a Sensor

The most effective bed bug surveillance network is your housekeeping team. We run mini-training for hotel staff:

  • Recognising bed bug signs — seam black dots (faecal spots), shed skins, blood stains, live insects; a paper clip and torch are enough for daily checks.
  • Luggage & high-risk spot checks — luggage racks, headboard backs and skirting where guest luggage touches.
  • Reporting workflow — how to report suspected findings, seal the room and preserve samples to prevent spread and repeat complaints.

Hotel Pest Control FAQ

What does a hotel pest control contract include?

Typically: scheduled guest-room and public-area inspections, bed bug rapid-response treatment, F&B area control, external monitoring, electronic reports for every visit, and year-round on-call arrangements. Frequency and scope are tailored to room count and rating — all in the written contract.

We found bed bugs in a guest room — how fast can you treat?

WhatsApp live during working hours for immediate scheduling. Flow: affected room sealed → adjacent rooms inspected (bed bugs spread through wall voids) → steam + crack-and-crevice treatment → follow-up. Arrival time depends on location and scheduling, confirmed on the day.

Will treatment disturb guests?

Discreet service: staggered scheduling (guest outings or late evening), unbranded equipment on request, fast and quiet works. Rooms return to service after the ventilation period — housekeeping keeps normal rooming.

What is Termatrac T3i and why does it matter for hotels?

T3i is a radar detection device that reads pest activity through walls and timber without demolition — marble, veneer and custom joinery stay untouched. It locates bed bug harbourages and termite nests alike: the first choice for non-destructive inspection.

Do you provide staff training?

Yes. Mini-training for housekeeping and front-line staff: recognising signs, checking luggage and high-risk spots, and the reporting workflow — turning the whole housekeeping team into a daily monitoring network.

Bed bugs come from guests — is the hotel liable?

Bed bugs primarily travel via guest luggage and clothing — a common mobility issue, not a hygiene failing. International hotels respond with "monitoring + rapid treatment + staff training". We recommend a reporting workflow with documented service records, so you have evidence if disputes arise.

Do you serve guesthouses, hostels and serviced apartments?

Yes. Hotels, guesthouses, hostels and serviced apartments across all 18 districts — programmes tailored to property size and risk, written quotation after survey.
