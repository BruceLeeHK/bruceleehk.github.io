FEHD Licensing & Inspections — Records That Keep You Ready

When FEHD licenses and inspects food premises, pest control measures and environmental hygiene are on the checklist. A complete treatment record is the most practical self-protection a restaurant can hold. We provide:

  • Electronic service reports — every visit lists locations, findings, treatment and follow-ups; digital filing, retrievable anytime.
  • IPM treatment records — methods and materials itemised for transparent inspection.
  • Monitoring trend records — monthly monitoring-point data with visible pest trends.
  • Honest note — our reports support your inspections; specific requirements ultimately rest with the inspecting department.

HACCP-aligned Monitoring — Food-safety-grade Methods

HACCP (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points) is the international food-safety framework, and pest control is one of its pillars. Our F&B programme aligns with it:

  • Monitoring point mapping — numbered monitoring points along kitchen flow, every check on record.
  • Trend records & corrective action — monthly data analysis; anomalies trigger immediate follow-up — a closed "monitor — correct" loop.
  • Non-chemical first — exclusion, trapping and environmental management lead; when needed, AFCD-registered pesticides in lockable bait stations away from food and prep surfaces.
  • Work protection — prep surfaces covered during treatment and cleaned afterwards; no contamination risk left behind.

F&B Pest Control FAQ

How much does a restaurant pest control contract cost?

Pricing depends on floor area, pest risk, service frequency and the number of monitoring points — restaurants vary widely. We survey first, then issue a written quotation listing scope, frequency, reporting arrangements and fee items. The written quotation is final.

What records does a FEHD inspection need? Can you provide them?

FEHD inspections review the premises' pest control measures. We provide electronic service reports, IPM treatment records and monitoring trend records for every case — filed and ready for inspection; specific requirements ultimately rest with the inspecting department.

What does "HACCP pest control" mean?

HACCP is the international food-safety management framework, and pest control is one of its pillars. We align with it: mapped monitoring points, trend records, corrective-action loops and non-chemical-first methods — connecting your pest programme to your food-safety system.

Will chemicals contaminate food?

We use food-safety-grade practice: non-chemical methods first (exclusion, trapping, environmental management); when pesticides are needed, AFCD-registered products in lockable bait stations positioned away from food and prep surfaces; prep surfaces covered during works and cleaned after.

Can you work at night? We cannot afford downtime.

Yes. After-closing night works are standard for our F&B clients — finished overnight, trading as usual next day. Larger works (e.g. major exclusion) are scheduled with you in advance.

Where do kitchen rodents usually enter?

Four routes: floor drains, rear door gaps (especially refuse-room doors), delivery windows and always-open doors, and goods deliveries. We exclude each with stainless-steel mesh, then verify with IoT rat boxes — source control, not just bait.

Do you have experience with Central and Mong Kok restaurants?

Yes. Our Central restaurant kitchen rodent case is fully published in the case-log series, and Mong Kok back-alley control has its own district page. We serve restaurants, food factories and delivery kitchens across all 18 districts.
