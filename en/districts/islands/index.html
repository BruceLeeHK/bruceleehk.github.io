Islands Pest Control FAQ

Is there a surcharge for island visits?

No. The Islands are a formal service zone at standard urban pricing — no ferry surcharge. Trips are pre-planned; WhatsApp for a quote first.

How long does a Cheung Chau village-house termite job take?

T3i scanning plus injection typically runs 2–4 hours depending on footprint and nest count; timing and process are confirmed before attendance.

Book lice breed endlessly on Lamma — is sea humidity the cause?

Directly. Book lice live on humidity and island flats stay moist; measure first, dehumidify, then treat crevices — that's the real cure.

How do we keep rats out in Tung Chung?

Kitchen drains, refuse-chute doors and external pipes are the big three entries; IoT mapping then proofing and trapping, with common-area improvement advice for management.
