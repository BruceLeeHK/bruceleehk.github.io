Why is Discovery Bay especially prone to termites and mosquitoes?

Discovery Bay's geography and property structure stack nearly every favourable condition for termites. Many residents only discover an infestation when floorboards bulge or door frames shed powder — because termites travel inside walls from the ground, invisible from the surface. Four key risk factors:

Wooded hillsides = rich colony sourcesSubterranean termite colonies breed in hillside soil, travelling through underground mud tubes into gardens, then up walls into timber.
Seafront humidity dampens timberSea mist and high humidity keep balcony railings, timber gates and eaves damp — damp wood is the Formosan termite's favourite food and water source.
Gardens directly against wallsVilla and village-house garden soil touches the external foundation directly; irrigation raises moisture at the wall base, letting termites climb in through foundation cracks.
Timber finishes + vacant periodsFalse ceilings, timber floors and cabinets are termite hideouts; owners based overseas often discover infestations late, when damage has already spread.

So the correct termite strategy for Discovery Bay is: non-invasive detection to locate the primary colony → residual termiticide injection → timber and moisture improvements (fix leaks, dehumidify, raise timber off ground) → periodic re-inspection. Surface spraying alone only diverts termite tunnels — they resurface months later in another room.
