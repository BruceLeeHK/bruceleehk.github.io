Why is Wong Tai Sin especially prone to Cockroaches?

In old estate plumbing, kitchens and bathrooms share drain lines — one infested unit endangers the whole pipe run. Ants build whole colonies behind walls and window frames, and sprays only kill the foraging workers while the queen keeps producing. Our pheromone baits travel back with the workers to poison the entire colony; roaches get standing smart bait stations; rats get IoT mapping before proofing and trapping — three pipe-borne pests, one integrated service.

Ants cover the whole kitchen edge — how do I finish them for good?

Kill the queen. Pheromone baits let workers carry the agent back to feed the whole colony — gone within days. Sprays only touch the surface and never end the colony.

Rats keep entering our Tsz Wan Shan flat — how to stop them?
