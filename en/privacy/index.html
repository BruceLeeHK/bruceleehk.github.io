2. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Essential storage (no consent required)

  • Cookie consent state (browser localStorage: blh_cookie_consent_v1) — remembers your banner choice so the notice does not re-appear on every visit.
  • Comment drafts — if a comment submission fails, your draft (nickname, text, photo preview) is temporarily kept in your own browser so you can retry; it is cleared automatically once posted.
  • Interface preferences — purely functional settings such as video playback state.

Analytics cookies (prior consent required)

This website loads Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager (GTM). Following the strict EU-GDPR "prior opt-in consent" standard: only when you press "Agree & Continue" on the cookie banner will the GA/GTM tracking code be loaded and activated. If you press "Essential Cookies Only", no GA/GTM code will ever be loaded and Google receives no data about your visit.

You can also re-open the banner any time via the "Cookie Settings" link in the website footer; or clear this website's cookies/site data in your browser settings and the banner will appear again on your next visit.

3. How Google Analytics Handles Your Data

Once you have consented, Google Analytics anonymously collects traffic data (page views, time on page, rough source region, device type) for the sole purpose of understanding which content is most helpful, so we can improve the structure and quality of this website. This data is not used to build personal profiles, and we do not configure GA for cross-site advertising tracking. For details on how Google processes analytics data, see the Google Privacy Policy; you may install the official Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on to disable it at any time.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Handling your enquiries, quote requests and on-site service bookings;
  • Publishing and replying to comments on the discussion board (only the nickname and content you provide are shown);
  • Improving website content and service quality using anonymised statistics;
  • Contacting you via the details you provided in urgent situations.

We will never sell, rent or disclose your personal data to any third party for profit, except where required by law or with your prior written consent.

5. Third-party Service Providers

This website uses the following third-party services, each handling the relevant data under its own privacy policy: Formspree (form delivery), Cloudflare (CDN, Web Analytics, Workers comment storage), Google Analytics (traffic analytics, loaded only after your consent) and cdnjs / Font Awesome (icon/font assets). We only provide each provider with the minimum data necessary to deliver the service.

6. Data Retention

  • Discussion board comments: kept until you request deletion or an administrator removes them under board rules;
  • Quotation form data: not reused for marketing once the follow-up is complete;
  • GA analytics data: expires automatically per Google Analytics default retention settings;
  • Cookie consent record: kept in your browser until you clear site data.

7. Your Rights (Hong Kong PDPO)

Under Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486), you have the right to ascertain whether we hold your personal data, to access that data, and to request correction of inaccurate data. To make an access, correction or deletion request, email master@bruceleehk.com and we will respond within a reasonable period (normally within 14 days). If you are dissatisfied with our privacy practices, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (PCPD) in Hong Kong.
