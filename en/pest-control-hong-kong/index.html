Yes. We only use pesticides registered with the AFCD (Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department), applied by licensed technicians. Safety data sheets (MSDS) are available on request.

How much does pest control cost in Hong Kong?

Single-visit treatments start from HK$800 — cockroaches from HK$900, rats from HK$1,500, termites from HK$2,800 and bed bugs from HK$3,000, depending on severity, floor area and the number of visits. Every quote is confirmed in writing after a free survey, with no hidden fees.

Yes — restaurants, hotels, offices, retail, food factories and property management. We provide FEHD-standard service reports to support your audits and HACCP compliance.
