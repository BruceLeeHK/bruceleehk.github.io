Hornet, Wasp or "Ma Fung"? The Naming Explained

In everyday Cantonese, "ma fung" (馬蜂), "wong fung" (黃蜂, wasp) and "wu fung" (胡蜂, hornet) are colloquial names that all point to the same stinging-insect family (Vespidae). Searching "hornet nest removal" or "wasp nest treatment" gets you the same professional service. What genuinely changes the approach is this:

🐝 Ma fung = wasp = hornet: colloquial, one removal approach 📄 Wasps: paper nests under eaves, branches, wall gaps 🍯 Bees: honeycomb nests — pollinators, relocate first ⚡ Disturbed wasps attack in swarms

So the first step is not the naming — it is keeping your distance and sending photos so a technician can tell hornet from bee. Not sure? Try our free AI pest image diagnosis.

Safety Principles — Species First, Environment Friendly

Nest removal is not just "knock it down". Species, gear, timing, prevention — all four matter:

  • Species ID first — hornets/wasps (paper nests, aggressive) vs bees (key pollinators) are handled completely differently; bee nests are referred for relocation, never blindly killed.
  • Professional gear — bee suits and telescopic tools for high and enclosed positions.
  • Timing — treatment at hours of lower wasp activity, with foot-traffic management, to reduce attack risk.
  • Re-nesting prevention — sealing + residue removal + seasonal checks to cut repeat nesting and repeat treatments.

Hornet & Wasp Nest FAQ

Is "ma fung" the same as "wasp"?

In everyday Cantonese, "ma fung" (馬蜂), "wong fung" (黃蜂, wasp) and "wu fung" (胡蜂, hornet) generally refer to the same family of stinging insects (Vespidae) — the removal approach is identical. What actually changes the method is the species check: paper nests point to wasps/hornets (aggressive), while honeycomb nests point to bees (key pollinators — we recommend contacting local beekeepers for relocation first).

I found a hornet nest at home. What should I do?

Do NOT knock it down, spray water, or burn it — disturbed colonies become highly aggressive. Keep your distance, take photos (no flash) and WhatsApp us; our technicians will assess the species and location before arranging treatment.

How much does hornet / wasp nest removal cost?

It depends on nest size, height and foot-traffic risk, so we do not publish a fixed price list; every job is quoted in writing after a site survey — no surprise add-ons. WhatsApp photos can get you a free preliminary assessment.

Who handles nests in estate common areas?

Nests in common areas (podiums, external walls, corridors) are generally the responsibility of the owners’ corporation or property management; we coordinate with management to schedule work away from peak foot traffic. Private premises (village houses, gardens) can book directly.

Is the DIY "burn the nest" trick safe?

No. Burning or hosing a nest is extremely dangerous — disturbed wasps attack in swarms and the colony may simply relocate somewhere hidden. Professional teams use bee suits, telescopic tools and proper timing; that is the reliable route.

Will wasps nest again after removal?

Old nests are not reused, but the same spot (eaves gaps, wall holes) can attract new colonies if conditions suit. After removal we provide re-nesting prevention advice: seal gaps, remove residue, seasonal checks.
